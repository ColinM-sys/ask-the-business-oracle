# Regional Sales Playbook (excerpt)

## When margin falls

1. Separate price effects (discounts, mix) from cost effects (COGS, freight).
2. Compare against the other regions in the same months: a shared cause shows everywhere.
3. Check discount programs against the policy caps and the approval trail.

## Levers

- Re-approve or end discount programs above the cap; move to targeted, deal-level discounts.
- Add a freight surcharge on Mid-Market and SMB orders where policy allows.
- Re-tender freight lanes; consolidate shipments of heavy categories.
- Shift mix toward higher-margin categories (Sensors, Valves).
