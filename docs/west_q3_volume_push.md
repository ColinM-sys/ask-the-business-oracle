# West: Q3 volume push (program note)

Owner: West regional sales. Approved by: Dana Ortiz (regional manager).

To defend share against a new competitor in the Pacific Northwest, West will offer discounts
of 15-22% on most deals from 1 July 2025. The program has no end date and will be reviewed
"when share stabilises".

Goal: grow West unit volume by 20% within two quarters.

Note: the program was set up at regional level. It was not sent to the VP Sales or to Finance
for a margin review.
