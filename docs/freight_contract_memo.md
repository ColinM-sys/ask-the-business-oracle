# Memo: West freight carrier change (June 2025)

From: Supply Chain. To: Regional managers.

From 1 July 2025 the West region moves from Norcal Freight to Pacific Haul Logistics. Norcal
exited the Pacific lanes, and Pacific Haul was the only carrier able to take the volume at
short notice. The contract is for 12 months with a break clause at 6 months.

Expected effect: freight per unit in West rises by roughly 55-65%. Supply Chain will re-tender
the West lanes once two regional carriers finish onboarding. Pumps are the most freight-heavy
category, because of their weight.

The other regions stay with Continental Freight on the existing rates.
