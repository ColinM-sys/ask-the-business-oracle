# Pricing and Discount Policy (rev. 2024-09)

Owner: Pricing Office. Applies to all regions.

## Discount authority

- A regional manager may approve discounts up to 10% off the invoiced unit price.
- Any discount above 10% needs written approval from the VP Sales before the quote is sent.
- A standing program (more than one quarter) above 10% also needs a margin review by Finance.

## Margin floor

- Target gross margin for every region is 35%.
- A region below 30% for two consecutive months triggers a pricing review within 30 days.

## Freight

- Freight is not passed to the customer on Enterprise contracts.
- On Mid-Market and SMB orders, a freight surcharge may be added when freight exceeds 5% of the line value.
